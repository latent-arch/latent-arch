OpenAI shipped [GPT-6 Astra](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/) on Thursday and since then my feed is about 70% Astra. Screenshots of it [driving a browser for forty minutes](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/gpt-6-astra-benchmarks-explained) without getting lost, Greg Brockman saying it is ["not unreasonable"](https://fortune.com/2026/09/03/openai-debuts-gpt-6-astra-computer-use-greg-brockman-says-start-of-agi/) to call this the AGI era, some guy [rebuilding a Blender scene](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-gpt-6-astra-openais-biggest) from one photo. Underneath that, the usual fight about whether the numbers mean anything. On the [aggregate intelligence index](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-6-astra) it barely moved past GPT-5.6. On agent and computer-use tasks it jumped a full generation. [Ten dollars per million input tokens, fifty per million output](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/3/gpt6-astra/). Paid users got it [a day later](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-6_Astra), in a version that refuses to discuss cybersecurity.

I have a habit with new models. Before reading a single benchmark table I ask them to draw a walrus playing a cello. Same prompt every time, one attempt, no retries. Two tusks, whiskers, a cello with two f-holes and four strings, standing on its endpin, bow in the flipper. Then I look at the picture. That's the whole method.

[Astra's walrus, next to fourteen others](/benchmarks/mascot-svg/#gpt-6-astra)

Every model on that page failed at the same spot: the bow. It hovers in the air, or fuses with the flipper, or goes straight through a tusk. Models knew what a walrus and a cello look like and still had no idea how one touches the other.

Astra's walrus is the first one where nothing is wrong. Both tusks, whiskers, a real cello with two f-holes, four strings, a bridge and a scroll, standing on its endpin. One flipper on the neck, the other holds the bow, and the bow is on the strings. First try, full checklist.

And I looked at it and felt nothing. It's a tidy cartoon, the kind you get from a stock icon pack. [Gemini's walrus](/benchmarks/mascot-svg/#gemini-3-1-pro) from February has a spotlight and closed eyes and clearly feels the music. Astra's just sits there doing everything correctly.

So, hype. On my walrus it's a good student. Not a new era.
