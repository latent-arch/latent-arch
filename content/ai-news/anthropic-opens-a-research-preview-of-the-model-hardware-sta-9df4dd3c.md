Anthropic opened a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a model-agnostic driver specification reachable over MCP that lets AI agents discover and safely operate physical lab devices with driver-enforced safety limits. Early adopters reported dramatic speedups, such as reducing instrument integration from weeks to hours and improving a laser relock success rate from 58% to 99.3%.
