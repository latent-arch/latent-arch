Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, both built on the same core model but split by access and safety mitigations, with the Cyber variant restricted to vetted defenders. The general model reasons more iteratively but may cost more due to higher token usage.
