Liquid AI released LFM2.5-2.6B, an open-weights on-device agentic model with 128K context and tool calling that runs entirely locally, decoding at 220 tokens/s on an M5 Max in under 2.5 GB. Weights ship in GGUF, MLX, and ONNX formats.
