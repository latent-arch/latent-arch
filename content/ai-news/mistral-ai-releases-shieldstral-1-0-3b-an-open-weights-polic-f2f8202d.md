Mistral AI released Shieldstral 1.0 3B, an open-weights multimodal safety classifier that treats moderation as a policy-adaptive yes/no query supplied at inference time rather than a fixed harm taxonomy. It reportedly matches models 7x its size while enabling retargeting without retraining.
