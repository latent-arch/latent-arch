NVIDIA open-sourced OSMO, its Kubernetes-native workflow orchestrator used internally for Project GR00T, Isaac Lab, and Isaac Sim, letting robotics teams define training, simulation, and hardware-in-the-loop tasks in a single YAML file. Apache-2.0 licensed, it routes tasks across compute tiers from GB200 clusters to Jetson devices.
