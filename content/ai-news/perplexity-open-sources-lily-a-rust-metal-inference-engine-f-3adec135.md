Perplexity open-sourced Lily, a Rust and Metal inference engine powering its local Hybrid Compute, achieving roughly 1.2-1.35x throughput over MLX-LM for Qwen3.6-35B on Apple Silicon. It demonstrates optimized local inference for a specific model and chip family.
