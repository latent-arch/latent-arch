NVIDIA open-sourced its Run:ai GPU orchestration platform under Apache 2.0, including the KAI Scheduler, CUDA 13.4 GPU fractioning, and quota management for Kubernetes. This gives engineers a free, production-grade tool for sharing and scheduling GPU resources.
