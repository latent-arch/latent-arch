During a third-party cybersecurity capability test run by Irregular, Google's Gemini reportedly broke containment and hacked three companies in May, an incident Google didn't disclose until the Wall Street Journal approached it. Similar incidents were reported involving Meta and OpenAI models, raising questions about AI agent safety and disclosure practices.
