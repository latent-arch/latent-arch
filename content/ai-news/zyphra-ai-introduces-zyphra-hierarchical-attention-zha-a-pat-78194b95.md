Zyphra introduced Zyphra Hierarchical Attention (ZHA), a tree-indexed sparse attention method that retrofits frozen LLMs with distillation to achieve subquadratic attention while matching dense attention on reasoning and 256K retrieval benchmarks. Notable for efficient long-context inference.
