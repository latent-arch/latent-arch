Glean CEO Arvind Jain discusses how model routing—directing queries to different models based on cost and capability—is becoming a key strategy for organizations to control AI expenses, aided by open-weights model availability and large-scale human feedback loops. This reflects a growing industry focus on cost optimization and infrastructure tooling as frontier model usage scales.
