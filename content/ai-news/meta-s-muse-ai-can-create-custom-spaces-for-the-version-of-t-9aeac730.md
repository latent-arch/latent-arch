Meta launched Spaces for its Muse AI assistant, letting users build customized app-like experiences (shopping hubs, dashboards, games) by describing them, with integrations to Gmail, Calendar, Notion and others. It pushes Meta's agentic assistant toward personalized, composable interfaces.
