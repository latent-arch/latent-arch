Google Research released ToolGrad, an answer-first framework for generating tool-use datasets that hits a 99.8% pass rate on ToolBench; a Gemma-3-12B fine-tuned on 500 samples nearly matched Gemini 2.5 Pro on BFCL. Code and models are Apache-2.0.
