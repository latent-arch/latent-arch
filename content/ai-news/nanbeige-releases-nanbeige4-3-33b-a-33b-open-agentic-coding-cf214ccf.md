Nanbeige LLM Lab released Nanbeige4.3-33B, a 33B dense Apache 2.0 agentic coding model with 512K context, reporting 80.9 on Terminal-Bench 2.1. The permissive license and strong coding benchmark make it notable for developers building agentic tools.
