NVIDIA released Alpamayo 2 Edge, a 2B-class reasoning vision-language-action model that plans and explains driving trajectories on DRIVE AGX Thor at 160-190ms pixel-to-trajectory latency. It targets on-vehicle autonomous driving with interpretable reasoning.
