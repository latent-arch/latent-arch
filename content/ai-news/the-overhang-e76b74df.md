Ethan Mollick's essay 'The Overhang' argues that current AI capabilities are underutilized, with meaningful value coming from applying deep and broad knowledge, taste, and agency rather than waiting for newer models. It's relevant for engineers thinking about how to extract more value from existing AI systems.
