AMD has acquired World Labs for $8.2B, with the company's Atlas system reportedly solving the sparse reconstruction problem for applications in robotics, design, and other spatial-intelligence domains. The deal marks a major consolidation move and signals AMD's expansion into 3D/spatial AI capabilities.
