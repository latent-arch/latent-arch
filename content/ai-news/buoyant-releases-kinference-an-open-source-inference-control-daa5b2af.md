Buoyant released Kinference, an Apache 2.0 open-source inference control plane for vLLM on Kubernetes featuring declarative routing, KV-cache-aware load balancing, and per-tenant metrics that cut median TTFT by 95% in its benchmark. It targets production LLM serving infrastructure.
