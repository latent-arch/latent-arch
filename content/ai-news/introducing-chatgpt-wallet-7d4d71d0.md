OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, letting agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and Dots complete purchases with user approval using existing payment cards, marking a step toward agentic commerce. Initially rolling out to ChatGPT Pro users in the US with eligible cards.
