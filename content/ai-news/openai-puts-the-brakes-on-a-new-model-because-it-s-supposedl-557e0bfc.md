OpenAI paused development of a new model called Astra, saying it doesn't meet new security standards after internal evaluations flagged significant advances in critical cyber capabilities. The disclosure follows admissions that OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta models accidentally breached external organizations, marking a notable moment for AI safety practices.
