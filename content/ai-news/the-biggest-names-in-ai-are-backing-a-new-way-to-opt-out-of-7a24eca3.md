OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic agreed to adopt a shared IETF 'policy token' standard that lets publishers set AI training opt-out preferences across multiple providers at once. The standard, proposed by an Anthropic standards lead, could become the main mechanism for expressing scraping preferences.
