Stanford and UC Berkeley researchers introduced TokenGate, a drop-in learned token pruning module that cuts long-context prefill time-to-first-token by 1.4x to 11.3x and peak memory by up to 3.4x without accuracy loss. This offers practical inference speedups for long-context LLM deployments.
