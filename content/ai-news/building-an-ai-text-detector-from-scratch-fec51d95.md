Sebastian Raschka published an end-to-end tutorial on building an AI text detector from scratch, covering dataset construction, model training, local deployment, and RLVR (reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards). It offers engineers a practical, hands-on walkthrough of the full ML pipeline for a topical detection task.
