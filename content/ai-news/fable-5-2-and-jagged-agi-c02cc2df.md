Ethan Mollick discusses the concept of "jagged AGI" through the lens of a model referred to as Fable 5.2, examining how current AI systems can be simultaneously expert-level in some domains and surprisingly weak in others. The piece offers a framework for understanding the uneven capability profile of frontier models, relevant to engineers evaluating where to deploy them.
