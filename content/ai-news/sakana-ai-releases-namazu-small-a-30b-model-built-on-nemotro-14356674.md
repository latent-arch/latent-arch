Sakana AI released Namazu Small, a 30B-A3B reasoning model built on NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Nano that averages 60.1 across 25 benchmarks, reportedly outperforming the larger 120B Nemotron 3 Super. It demonstrates strong efficiency gains in smaller reasoning models.
