HKU and ByteDance Seed researchers open-sourced Laminar, an asynchronous RL post-training framework that decouples rollout, training, and weight sync, lifting Qwen3-Coder-30B to 52.2% on SWE-bench Verified. It advances efficient RL fine-tuning tooling for coding models.
