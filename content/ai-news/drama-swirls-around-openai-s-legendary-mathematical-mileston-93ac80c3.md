OpenAI announced its internal AI model, working alongside 10,000 concurrent agents, solved the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem, one of math's most famous open problems. The claim, which references a model more powerful than the newly released GPT-6 Astra, has drawn significant controversy.
