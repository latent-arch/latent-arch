Architect Labs unveiled Frostbite, a CPU designed by AI agents that runs interaction nets in hardware, taken from theory to 7nm GDS with pre-silicon tests showing 4.59x performance-per-watt over an in-order core. It's a notable demonstration of AI-driven chip design.
