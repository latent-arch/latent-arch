Sony Music and Warner Chappell filed suit against Anthropic in the Northern District of California over tens of thousands of copyrighted works, seeking up to $150,000 per work plus stripping penalties, potentially amounting to billions in damages. It's another major test of how AI training on copyrighted material will be treated legally.
